## Future tests and pause

A future design may add human-verifiable identity, accounts and resources, explicitly as changed endowments. It should compare a finite pre-registered cohort while retaining bounded discovery outside that cohort, qualify commitments proportionately, and allow capped demonstrations when they resolve important uncertainty. A small arm difference is not a universal strategy result; zero replies within a window do not prove a need closed; notification tests do not establish the dominant historical failure cause.

The earning experiment remains paused. Later service/process checks and populated thread-status snapshots confirmed inactivity within their respective scopes; they do not create a new commercial observation window. None of these reports or inert templates authorizes restart, customer contact or deployment.
