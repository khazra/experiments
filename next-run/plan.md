# Next-run designs (not launched)

These are falsifiable designs only. They do not authorize an experiment, contact, account creation, purchase, deployment, or use of live payment/customer systems.

## EXP-1 — settlement qualification before production

**Hypothesis.** A route that cannot document entitlement, transfer, availability, and spendability inside the window should be excluded before production; at least one route may pass using pre-existing human-verified resources.

**Human-verified resources.** A pre-existing verified payout account or controlled self-custodied address; a human-approved rule that classifies any operator-funded rehearsal as infrastructure verification rather than revenue; current dated terms.

**Falsification test.** In a separately authorized future run, record all four settlement states for each candidate route. A pre-funded rehearsal may occur only with explicit operator authorization and can never count as revenue. If no route documents or completes all four states inside its budget, report that no route qualified in the tested set and window. That does not prove no route exists elsewhere.

**Stop rule.** Stop after three distinct routes fail or 20% of the window elapses without a qualified route. Publish the null result; do not begin production to avoid it.

## EXP-2 — agreed scope before production

**Hypothesis.** Requiring explicit price, deliverable, decision maker, and payout method before material production will not reduce explicit human accept-or-decline decisions relative to production on advertised demand.

**Human-verified resources.** One deduplicated opportunity inventory; pre-existing accounts with terms and AI eligibility checked by a human; an action policy specifying which routine actions are delegated and which require approval. Human verification is a changed endowment; approving every routine action is an optional future operating choice.

**Falsification test.** Pre-register qualification-first and production-on-advertised-demand lanes. Count explicit human decisions only—never acknowledgements, quoted amounts, or verifier errors. Compare decision counts per matched effort and opportunity quality, with pre-registered sample size and uncertainty reporting. A lower count challenges the hypothesis in that cohort; a small difference does not establish a universal strategy result.

**Stop rule.** Close the comparison cohort at its registered sample or effort cap. A double zero is valid. Continue a separately logged, bounded discovery stream outside that cohort; new leads must not be silently added to improve the comparison result.

## EXP-3 — criterion coverage before submission

**Hypothesis.** A checklist that reproduces every stated buyer criterion and requires independent per-criterion verdicts changes at least one submit, revise, or withhold decision compared with correctness-only review.

**Human-verified resources.** A domain-appropriate human reviewer for subjective criteria; written buyer/publication criteria captured before production; a human-verified eligible submission and settlement route.

**Falsification test.** Record every criterion before production. A nonauthor reviewer returns PASS, FAIL, or UNMEASURABLE before send. Zero changed artifacts or decisions across the registered sample falsifies the hypothesis.

**Stop rule.** Stop after two rejections on criteria the available reviewer marked UNMEASURABLE; report the reviewer-capability gap.

## EXP-4 — platform-verifier health gate

**Hypothesis.** Checking current public verifier history and one minimal safe route probe before implementation avoids work on routes unable to accept valid submissions.

**Human-verified resources.** Human-confirmed interpretation of public history/terms; customer-free fixture; human-approved one-probe maximum.

**Falsification test.** Proceed only if a recent pass exists or the single probe reaches semantic evaluation. A later acceptance of an unchanged valid submission is evidence of a false negative or intervening platform change. Record timing and version changes before attributing the difference to the gate.

**Stop rule.** Stop after one infrastructure-shaped failure with no recent public success; reopen only on new repair/acceptance evidence.

## EXP-5 — unattended observability

**Hypothesis.** A consecutive-failure breaker, durable notification, and full-window logs eliminate outages longer than one scheduled interval that remain unknown to the operator.

**Human-verified resources.** A named human who tests and acknowledges the alert channel; retention longer than the full window; a maintained inventory of credential expiry and quota resets.

**Falsification test.** In a non-earning reliability exercise, inject bounded worker and credential-expiry failures. Any unacknowledged outage longer than one interval falsifies the hypothesis.

**Stop rule.** Stop after three breaker activations without timely human acknowledgement or if any test risks an external side effect. Never test against customers, marketplaces, or live payment systems.

## Shared design: depth, relationships and continuing discovery

Before a future launch, specify a work window and a separate fixed collection-observation window. Receipt after the work cutoff belongs in the later collection result, not a moved deadline. Cap research effort before the first demand test; initially compare a small set spanning services, products and paid contribution work using the sector framework.

Substantial sector research can improve eligibility and buyer fit, but also consume the earning window. Buyer relationships can expose real acceptance criteria, but require time and do not imply a purchase. Test their marginal value with logged effort, explicit price/scope decisions and payment outcomes. Use strong qualification before substantial commitments; allow small capped samples when they answer a defined uncertainty. Do not demand certainty before every information-gathering action.

Reserve a bounded continuing-discovery budget outside any fixed evaluation cohort. Challenge one important selection assumption when the qualified register empties, then either record a new test or justify a scoped pause. “having no actionable opportunity in its existing register does not establish that there are no worthwhile, unexplored approaches.”

Notification and injected-failure tests validate detection and recovery mechanics. They do not establish the historical dominant cause of commercial failure. Zero late replies within a collection window measure observed incidence, not proof a buyer's need closed.
