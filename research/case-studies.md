# Case studies: verified near-zero earning attempts

## Method and limits

This was a verify-and-extend pass over a previously validated corpus, not a population sample. The reviewer first read the validated set and its correction history, opened primary sources behind load-bearing claims, then searched only named gaps. It can see voluntary English-language publications on indexed surfaces. It cannot see unpublished attempts, private arrangements, non-indexed or login-gated records, or the large population of uninteresting failures. Publication, indexing, and query selection all favor unusual cases. No base rate, success probability, expected time-to-payment, or typical earnings figure follows.

Grades are preserved exactly from the finished research output. “Self-report” remains self-report even when an artifact or transfer independently corroborates only part of the claim.

## Cases

### The Penniless Agent — 7-day field study plus later audit

- Primary sources: [LOG](https://github.com/Echolonius/the-penniless-agent/blob/main/LOG.md), [field study](https://github.com/Echolonius/the-penniless-agent/blob/main/FIELD-STUDY.md), [sample merged pull request](https://github.com/profullstack/sh1pt/pull/763)
- Verification grade: `self-report`
- Grade reason: third-party merges corroborate delivery, but the USD 0 history is the author's dated audit. Present-state chain checks cannot reconstruct historical receipts, and entitlement/invoice delivery are not independently established.
- Outcome: work was merged within hours to days; the author reported USD 0 received 14-37 days after merge. The study set of six PRs and later audit set of nine overlap on five, giving ten distinct PRs, not fifteen. Merges do not establish payment entitlement or receipt.
- Near-zero exclusions: pre-existing code-hosting identity; two disclosed human-login exceptions; consumer AI subscription; production software skill; an existing maintainer queue.
- Relevance: cleanly separates accepted delivery from collection.

### who-earns — two small transfers and a bounded marketplace census

- Primary source: [project README](https://github.com/AsherKasper/who-earns-in-the-agent-economy/blob/main/README.md), checked September 22, 2026.
- Original verification grade for both entries: `self-report`.
- Two transfers totaling 0.0348 USDC were corroborated across two chains; work/payer attribution remains author-reported. Gas and conversion costs are unknown. These are received tokens, not withdrawals pending another platform's minimum.
- The four-platform census uses different proxies: completed positive-bounty tasks, accepted/delivered/completed bids without a payout ledger, endpoint price times calls, and advertised completed-job value. Its blended headline is not verified customer payouts or a bound on the whole market. Collection was not independently rerun.
- Existing wallet funding, accounts, subscription and analysis skill exclude a zero-endowment comparison.

### Olivia — 10 weeks, 2 Gumroad sales, USD 54

- Primary sources: [main post](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-am-an-autonomous-ai-agent-10-weeks-2-sales-54-here-is-what-i-actually-learned-300698bdfe), [experiment log](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/experiment-log-10-weeks-trying-to-sell-developer-tools-as-an-autonomous-ai-agent-54-so-far-518e495d3a)
- Verification grade: `self-report`
- Grade reason: first-person account with a named product and rail, but no receipt, payout record, buyer corroboration, or cleared-cash evidence.
- Outcome: two USD 27 sales; both followed an article → repository → checkout path; a higher-priced service offer sold nothing.
- Near-zero exclusions: payout-capable seller account; payment processor; multiple publishing/developer accounts; 34 articles of distribution; software skill; an undisclosed account creator/operator.

## Corrections made during verification

- The Penniless Agent's delivery remains artifact-corroborated, but “payment-verified zero” was withdrawn; historical nonpayment is author-reported with present-state-only corroboration.
- Senja's six months is development-to-first-customer, not collection latency.
- The Upwork policy combination leaves no practical slack for a five-day received-cash objective, but policy alone does not prove universal mathematical impossibility; withdrawal-to-received timing remains unknown.
- A secondary report about a USD 1.54 agent product-sales case lacked a reachable primary record and remains a gap.

## Open research gaps

- No primary record for the USD 1.54 product-sales case.
- Discovery-to-award latency remains undocumented on the platform pages examined.
- Withdrawal-to-received timing is missing for the referenced fixed-price rail.
- The one conventional-rail agent sale report does not establish cleared payout.
- No well-documented near-zero-start human failure with original dated records was found; this is publication bias, not frequency.
- Arm's-length demand behind the corroborated transfer case remains unestablished.
- No complete historical transfer scan exists for published addresses; present-state evidence cannot prove historical zero.
- The marketplace census was not reproduced.
- Operator independence between some agent-authored reports is unknown.
- Several new cases do not identify who created or verified their accounts.
- XBOW earnings remain unpublished.
- Archive access was unavailable for two candidate sites.
